Not HMRC-recognised tax software. CIS Ledger does not file CIS300 monthly returns, does not file Self Assessment, and does not talk to HMRC. It produces records you keep. You still need your contractor’s payment and deduction statement, and an accountant or HMRC for tax due.

What it does

  • Invoice a UK contractor with labour / materials / plant / other lines.
  • CIS on labour and other CIS-able work only — not materials, not plant hire, not VAT. Rates 0%, 20%, 30% as HMRC verification sets.
  • A4 PDF the accounts team can pay (bank details, CIS line, net, VAT if you charge it).
  • Monthly payment-and-deduction record to check against the contractor’s CIS statement.
  • CSV for your accountant. JSON backup so you do not lose the book.
  • UK dates (DD/MM/YYYY), pounds, tax months from the 6th to the 5th.

What it does not do

  • Not recognised by HMRC as tax software.
  • Does not file CIS300s or Self Assessment.
  • Does not verify you with HMRC or tell you which CIS rate you “should” be on — that is the contractor’s verification.
  • Does not advise on VAT domestic reverse charge. Ask your accountant.
  • Does not replace the contractor’s official statement (they must still send one within 14 days of the tax month end).
  • Does not store data in the cloud. v1 is this browser, this device.
